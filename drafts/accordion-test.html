Frequently Asked Questions

What is Edge Delivery Services?

Edge Delivery Services is Adobe's approach to building fast, easy-to-author websites on top of AEM as a Cloud Service.

How do I author content for a block?

Content is authored in Google Docs or Word documents, and structured using tables that map to blocks like this one.

Where did this block come from?

It was adapted from the AEM Block Collection, also known as Block Party.
